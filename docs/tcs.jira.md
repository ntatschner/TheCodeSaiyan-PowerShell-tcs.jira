## tcs.jira Cmdlets
### [Clear-JiraContext](Clear-JiraContext.md)
Removes the Jira connection context and credential from the current session.

### [Find-JiraIssue](Find-JiraIssue.md)
Finds Jira Cloud issues with a JQL query.

### [Get-JiraContext](Get-JiraContext.md)
Gets the Jira connection context of the current session, without secrets.

### [Get-JiraIssueTransition](Get-JiraIssueTransition.md)
Gets the transitions that can be performed on a Jira Cloud issue.

### [Get-JiraTicket](Get-JiraTicket.md)
Gets a Jira Cloud issue with its comments.

### [Get-JSMRequest](Get-JSMRequest.md)
Gets a Jira Service Management customer request.

### [Get-JSMRequestTransition](Get-JSMRequestTransition.md)
Gets the customer transitions of a Jira Service Management request.

### [Get-JSMRequestType](Get-JSMRequestType.md)
Gets the request types of a Jira Service Management service desk.

### [Get-JSMServiceDesk](Get-JSMServiceDesk.md)
Gets Jira Service Management service desks.

### [Invoke-JiraIssueTransition](Invoke-JiraIssueTransition.md)
Moves a Jira Cloud issue to a status, optionally with a comment.

### [Invoke-JiraRequest](Invoke-JiraRequest.md)
Sends a request to the Jira Cloud or Jira Service Management REST API.

### [New-JiraTicket](New-JiraTicket.md)
Creates a Jira Cloud issue.

### [New-JSMRequest](New-JSMRequest.md)
Creates a Jira Service Management customer request.

### [Set-JiraContext](Set-JiraContext.md)
Sets the Jira Cloud site and credentials used by the other tcs.jira functions.

### [Set-JSMRequestTransition](Set-JSMRequestTransition.md)
Transitions a Jira Service Management request to a new status.

### [Test-JiraContext](Test-JiraContext.md)
Checks that the Jira context works by getting the signed-in user.

### [Update-JiraTicket](Update-JiraTicket.md)
Updates a Jira Cloud issue: transitions it, changes fields and/or adds a comment.

### [Update-JSMRequest](Update-JSMRequest.md)
Updates a Jira Service Management request: marks it done, changes fields and/or adds a comment.
